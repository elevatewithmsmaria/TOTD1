1. What is Training? & Differences

Training: A structured process of developing knowledge, skills, and attitudes to improve performance.

Information ≠ Learning ≠ Performance

  • Teaching: Helping people understand knowledge.
  • Training: Developing knowledge & skills for performance.
  • Coaching: Guidance & practice to improve.
  • Facilitation: Helping a group learn, discuss & solve problems.
  • Mentoring: Sharing experience for long-term growth.

2. The ADDIE Cycle & 5 Trainer Roles

ADDIE Framework:

Analyze → Design → Develop → Implement → Evaluate

5 Core Trainer Roles:

  • 🎤 Instructor: Provides knowledge & demonstrates skills.
  • 🤝 Facilitator: Creates participation & discussion.
  • 🧭 Coach: Helps learners improve via feedback.
  • 📋 Assessor: Checks if competency is achieved.
  • 📊 Evaluator: Determines if training worked.

3. Andragogy (Knowles' 5 Principles)

  • Self-Concept: Adults want control over learning.
  • Experience: Adults bring prior knowledge.
  • Readiness: Connects to current roles/needs.
  • Orientation: Problem-centered learning.
  • Internal Motivation: Driven by personal growth/satisfaction.
WIIFM: "What's In It For Me?" - Connect content directly to personal value!

4. Retention & Multi-Method Delivery

Multi-Method Flow:

Explain → Show → Discuss → Practice → Reflect

Retention Strategies:

  • Spacing: Review information over time.
  • Retrieval Practice: Active recall vs simple reading.
  • Storytelling: Memorable scenarios.
  • Application: Real work connection.
