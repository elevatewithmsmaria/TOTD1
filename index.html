1. What is Training & Roles Comparison

Training: Structured process of developing knowledge, skills, and attitudes to improve performance.

Information ≠ Learning ≠ Performance

  • Teaching: Helping understand knowledge.
  • Training: Developing knowledge & skills for performance.
  • Coaching: Guidance & practice to improve.
  • Facilitation: Group discussion & problem solving.
  • Mentoring: Sharing experience for long-term growth.

2. ADDIE Framework & 5 Core Hats

Analyze → Design → Develop → Implement → Evaluate

  • 🎤 Instructor: Knowledge & skill demonstration.
  • 🤝 Facilitator: Drives discussion & participation.
  • 🧭 Coach: Feedback & guided improvement.
  • 📋 Assessor: Checks individual competency.
  • 📊 Evaluator: Assesses overall training effectiveness.

3. Andragogy & WIIFM

  • Self-Concept: Adults desire self-direction.
  • Experience: Adults bring existing knowledge.
  • Readiness: Learning tied to current roles.
  • Orientation: Problem-centered application.
  • Internal Motivation: Driven by personal growth.
WIIFM: "What's In It For Me?" - Hook relevance early!

4. Multi-Method & Memory Retention

Explain → Show → Discuss → Practice → Reflect

  • Spacing: Periodic review over time.
  • Retrieval Practice: Active memory recall.
  • Storytelling: Contextual narrative hooks.
  • Application: Direct job connection.
